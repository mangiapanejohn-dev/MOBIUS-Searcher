# Code of Conduct

MØBIUS-Searcher is an engineering and research project. Discussion should make the work easier to verify, reproduce, review, and improve.

## Expected

- Critique code, measurements, assumptions, and claims directly.
- Separate measured facts from guesses and opinions.
- Provide reproduction details when reporting technical problems.
- Treat contributors with basic respect regardless of experience level.
- Accept negative results and failed hypotheses as useful evidence.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, targeted insults, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Intentionally misleading benchmark or profit claims.
- Sharing secrets, wallet keys, credentials, or sensitive user data.
- Spam, artificial engagement, or promotional content unrelated to the project.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable and safe.

For security-sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting publicly.
